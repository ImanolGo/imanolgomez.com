## What this site stores on your device

One entry in `localStorage`, under the key `theme`, holding either `light` or `dark`.
It records the colour theme you picked with the toggle so the site does not flip back
on the next page load. It contains no identifier and is never transmitted anywhere.
It is set only if you click the toggle, and clearing your browser data removes it.

There are no cookies.

This site links to external websites (GitHub, LinkedIn and others). Following a link

loaded from those domains while you are here, so no data reaches them until you click.
