I'm a technical lead and AI engineer based in Berlin. I've spent 15+ years building
software, starting with interactive art and live performance before moving into medical
devices and automotive AI. I lead engineering teams and work directly with customers
and developers, from the first requirements through to production.

## Current work

I'm Head of AI Deployment at CARIAD, the Volkswagen Group software company, where I
lead 12 engineers taking AI models from research prototypes to automotive hardware. I work
with engineering teams, Volkswagen Group brands and semiconductor partners to integrate
models, meet performance and reliability requirements, and deliver releases. Our
deployments target QNX.

Before leading the team, I built a Python SDK for model conversion and optimization,
and a runtime abstraction layer used by other engineering teams. I worked with those
teams from their first requirements through production integration, and enjoyed seeing
the tools become part of their day-to-day work.

At NELA by Carepath Technologies, I worked on model design, device firmware (an RTOS on
microcontrollers) and IoT architecture for an AI-based tele-monitoring product for chronic-disease patients.
That work ran from requirements to series production in a regulated healthcare environment.

## Embedded and real-time work

My background is in telecommunication engineering and sound and music computing. For most
of my career I have written bare-metal microcontroller firmware and real-time software for
lighting, wearables and installations, alongside interactive and video applications running
at 30 FPS or more on the GPU under Linux. At
Ars Electronica in Linz, I built interactive computer-vision installations and led
development of GeoPulse, a spatial-data visualization platform.

I then spent six years as a freelance creative technologist. I built lighting products
for ProtoPixel, wearables with ElektroCouture and installations with Moritz Waldemeyer.
For Aakash Odedra Company's *Murmur*, I engineered the show software and operated it
on the world tour. Alongside that work, I taught, gave talks and ran workshops and
hackathons, from Barcelona and Berlin to Bangkok and Accra.

## Personal projects

Outside work, I build [Alibi](@/projects/alibi/index.md), a murder-mystery game with LLM
suspects, memory and automated evaluations, and I measured [how many AI agents one GPU
can serve](@/projects/agents-per-gpu/index.md) on vLLM, SGLang and TensorRT-LLM. My [on-device robot assistant](@/projects/on-device-robot-assistant/index.md)
combines voice and vision without the cloud. I also write [GPU flocking simulations](@/projects/murmuration-gpu/index.md)
in Rust, and I recently wrote one flock step four ways (CUDA C++, cuda-oxide, wgpu and
cuTile) and [profiled them with Nsight](@/projects/murmuration-kernels/index.md). My main languages are Python and C++; I use Rust for personal projects.

## How I work

I like to put a working prototype in front of the people who will use it early.
Their feedback, along with benchmarks and evaluations, helps me decide what to keep.
I use shared tools, documentation and mentoring to help other engineers ship their work.
I plan for testing, performance and traceability from the start.

Away from the keyboard, I'm into sports and nutrition. I'm vegan and care about animals
and the environment.
