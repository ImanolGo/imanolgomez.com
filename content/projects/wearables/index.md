## Context

I worked on contract with ElektroCouture, the Berlin fashion-tech studio, on and
off between 2016 and 2018. I led design and development of its light-emitting
garments and was the main developer of the driver firmware. The LED wearables
had to work while being worn and danced in, and when photographed on stage.

The pieces included FireFly, Nuit Blanche and Marlene during a two-year stretch of
shows and commissions. Later work included Norma at the Greek National Opera in
2019, and long-exposure light-painting fashion photography with the designer
Anja Dragan.

## The problem

The electronics have to fit inside fabric, run from a small battery and keep
working as the wearer moves. A dropped frame can show up on camera. The driver
hardware needs to stay cool and close to the size of a coin, while its firmware
drives many LEDs at a stable refresh rate.

The tools also have to support designers making visual changes in the studio,
sometimes only hours before a show.

## What I did

- Led design and development of the wearable products themselves, hardware and
  firmware together.
- Wrote and maintained the wearable driver firmware for LED driving, sensor input and
  the animation pipeline.
- Worked alongside designers and textile technologists on pieces that had to survive
  being worn, including the Forster Rohner LED textile work behind **Marlene**.
- Mentored on fashion-tech projects and workshops.
